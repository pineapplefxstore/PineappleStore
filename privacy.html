Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pineapple Store ("we," "us," or "PineappleFX") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website https://www.pineapplestore.us.ci/, purchase PineappleFX products, or interact with our support services.

2. Information We Collect
  • Account & contact information: name, email address, Telegram username, and Telegram User ID when you create an account or contact support.
  • Transaction information: payment details processed through Gumroad or Stripe (we do not store full credit card numbers), order ID, and purchase amount.
  • Technical information: your Machine ID (Computer ID) — an 8-character identifier generated by the PineappleFX After Effects panel — used exclusively for license binding and activation.
  • Usage data: pages visited, referrer, and browser type for analytics and security.
3. How We Use Your Information
  • To generate, deliver, and verify your PineappleFX license key.
  • To process payments and issue receipts through Gumroad / Stripe.
  • To provide customer support via our Telegram support bot.
  • To send product updates, security notices, and license-related communications.
  • To prevent fraud and enforce our license terms.
4. Third-Party Services
  • Payments: Gumroad and Stripe handle payment processing. Please review their privacy policies for details on how they handle payment data.
  • Support: Our Telegram support bot (@PineappleFxstore_bot) receives your chat ID and messages to track support tickets.
  • Analytics: We may use basic analytics to understand site traffic and improve performance.
5. Data Retention & Security
  • License keys and Machine IDs are retained for as long as your license is active.
  • Support tickets are retained for 12 months to assist with follow-up requests.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest where applicable.
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We do not sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to third parties except as described above or as required by law.
6. Your Rights
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it for processing.
  • Contact us regarding license reactivation, transfer, or deletion after a machine change.
7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use session cookies to maintain your login state and optional analytics cookies to improve the website. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function properly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us via our Telegram support bot: @PineappleFxstore_bot or email pineapplefx.store@gmail.com.
